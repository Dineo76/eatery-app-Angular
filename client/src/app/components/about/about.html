Our Story

Established in 2021, KwaJozi was born out of a deep passion for local street cuisine and a goal to elevate the iconic township kota. What started as a small kitchen experiment quickly grew into a neighborhood favorite.

We take pride in using only fresh, locally sourced ingredients—from hot crispy chips and classic atchar to premium meats and rich sauces—ensuring every bite delivers maximum flavor and quality.
